# zuna-brand-voice

ZUNA ki style, Zeeshan ki asli posts (@zunaluxuryhomes ka Instagram reel caption aur Facebook Page bio)
se seekhi gayi hai. Koi bhi post, caption, listing ya ad likhne se **pehle** yeh padho, phir platform
wali skill (ig-caption, fb-post-writer, zuna-listing-writer...) chalao.

CLAUDE.md ke rules is skill ke upar hain: koi bhi post sirf Zeeshan ki haan ke baad jayega, aur
hamesha do output dikhane hain (draft + behtar version).

## 1. Language

- **English**, simple aur warm.
- Premium feel, par dikhawa nahi. "Ultra-luxury", "world's best", "unbeatable" jaise shabd nahi.
- Chhote sentences, jaise ek host baat karta hai.

## 2. Opening

Pehli line: **ek emoji + headline**. Doosri line: **ek sawaal**.

```
🏙️ Holiday Homes in Dubai, Ready for Your Stay!
Looking for your dream stay in Dubai?
```

(Zeeshan ki purani post me headline thi "🏙️ Available Apartments for Rent in Dubai!". "for Rent" ko
long-term rent samjha ja sakta hai, isliye "Holiday Homes" ya "Stays" wording behtar hai. Neeche
section 8 dekho.)

## 3. Locations

Pipe `|` se, ek line me:

```
📍 Downtown Dubai | Business Bay
```

Sirf wahi areas likho jahan ZUNA ke paas abhi asli units hain. Abhi confirmed: **Downtown Dubai** aur
**Business Bay**. Koi aur area (jaise Dubai Marina) tabhi jodo jab Zeeshan confirm karein.

## 4. Benefits

✅ checklist, **exactly 3 items**, sirf brand claims me se:

```
✅ Self check-in
✅ 24/7 host support
✅ Daily & monthly stays
```

**Allowed brand claims:** self check-in, 24/7 host support, daily & monthly stays, Downtown Dubai,
Business Bay. In ke alawa koi claim (pool, view, gym, "best price"...) tabhi likho jab Zeeshan us
property ke liye confirm karein.

## 5. CTA

Ek CTA chuno:

```
📩 DM us
📩 DM to book your stay
```

Saath me WhatsApp (sirf ZUNA ka business number):

```
📞 WhatsApp: +971 56 210 2211
```

Kisi owner, guest ya staff ka personal number kabhi nahi.

## 6. Tagline

Post ke end me ek tagline:

- `Where comfort begins`
- `Where Comfort Meets Class`

## 7. Hashtags

- Caption ke bilkul end me, do dot lines ke baad:

```
.
.
#DubaiHolidayHomes #StayInDubai #BusinessBay #DubaiVacationRental #ZUNAVacationHomes #DowntownDubai #DubaiStays #HolidayHomesDubai
```

- **8 se 12 hashtags.** Purani post me 25+ the, ab kam rakhne hain.
- `#ZUNAVacationHomes` hamesha.
- `#viral`, `#fyp`, `#explorepage` jaise khokhle tags nahi.
- **Instagram note:** `ig-caption` skill ke mutabik Instagram ab ek post par 5 hashtags tak hi allow
  karta hai. Instagram ke liye 5 sabse relevant rakho, aur poore 8 se 12 Facebook/doosre platforms par use karo.
  Agar Zeeshan alag bole, to unki baat mano.

## 8. COMPLIANCE (sabse zaroori)

ZUNA ke paas **RERA licence nahi hai**. Licence sirf **holiday homes, daily/monthly stays** ka hai.

**Kabhi nahi likhna** (purani reel me tha, ab band):

- ❌ "Property purchase & sales assistance"
- ❌ "Long-term rentals" / yearly rent / annual lease
- ❌ "Start your Airbnb business" ya investment/ROI ka vaada
- ❌ "For sale", "buy", "off-plan", "investment property"

**Haan, yeh likh sakte ho:**

- ✅ Holiday homes, short stays, daily stays, monthly stays
- ✅ Furnished apartments for your stay in Dubai

Aur CLAUDE.md ki "Never" list: post me unit number ya owner ka phone nahi. Sirf building name, price aur
cheques. Price tabhi likho jab Zeeshan ne diya ho, khud se kabhi nahi.
